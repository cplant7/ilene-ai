Solution 1 of 5

AI Audits & Assessments

Most leaders cannot say whether they are ready for AI, or what their current AI is costing or returning. Before you buy, and once AI is in use, we assess readiness, maturity, risk and spend so you know where to invest, what to fix and what to stop.

What we do

  • Readiness before an AI purchase: the problem, workflow, data, ownership and controls
  • Current AI use, including unmanaged or shadow AI
  • Workflow, data and integration gaps that block scale
  • Ownership, human accountability and governance maturity
  • A use case inventory scored for value, feasibility and risk
  • Baseline measures for revenue, cost, productivity or customer impact

Typical outputs

  • Readiness verdictWhether you are ready to buy, and what to fix before you do
  • Maturity stage and heat mapWhere you stand today, dimension by dimension
  • Priority gaps and risk registerWhat is blocking value, and what could go wrong
  • Ranked use casesWhere to invest first, scored for value, effort and risk
  • Preliminary ROI caseThe likely return, and the assumptions behind it
  • 90-day action planWhat to do next, plus a clear “not now” list

Solution 2 of 5

AI Transformation

AI added on top of old processes adds cost without adding value. We redesign how the work gets done and plan the change, so AI is built into the workflow from the start. AI Implementation then puts the plan into daily use. Clients who want end-to-end support combine the two.

What we do

  • Map the current process and remove unnecessary effort
  • Design the target workflow around the right mix of people, automation and AI
  • Define human review, escalation and exception handling
  • Set roles and responsibilities in the new workflow
  • Plan adoption and the move into implementation

Typical outputs

  • Target workflowHow the work runs with AI built in, step by step
  • Operating modelWho does what, and where people stay in the loop
  • Adoption planHow teams move to the new way of working
  • KPI definitionsThe measures that show whether the change is paying off
  • Implementation planWhat to put into daily use first, and in what order
Solution 3 of 5

AI Governance

If AI tools go into daily work before anyone owns decisions, data access and controls, you get unclear accountability, risk exposure and adoption that stalls. Fixing that afterwards costs more than setting it up first. We put clear owners, decision rights and practical controls in place before rollout, sized to the work.

What we do

  • A governance model sized to your organization
  • Decision rights and an accountable owner for each AI use
  • Controls, guardrails and human review points
  • Escalation paths and monitoring requirements
  • A review cadence leadership will actually keep

Typical outputs

  • Governance modelA structure sized to your organization, not a binder
  • Decision-rights mapWho approves, owns and answers for each AI use
  • Control pointsWhere people review, escalate and stop
  • Risk registerKnown risks with an owner and a response for each
  • Review cadenceA rhythm leadership will actually keep. Ongoing control ownership stays with your leaders.
Solution 4 of 5

AI Implementation

AI only pays once it is in daily use. Long pilots and big-bang rollouts delay the return and stall adoption. We start where the solution can be applied first, put it to work in short, focused stages and measure the results as they come in, so each stage builds on the last.

What we do

  • A first use chosen because it can be applied quickly and measured
  • Business and technical requirements, kept to what the first stage needs
  • Vendor evaluation across data and security, integration, model transparency, terms, support and roadmap
  • A rollout in short stages, each with clear success gates
  • Configuration and integration coordination with your teams and partners
  • Change, training and operating documentation

Typical outputs

  • First use in daily workApplied, measured and ready to build on
  • Clear recommendationThe solution to choose, and why, backed by a weighted vendor scorecard
  • Staged roadmapWhat comes next, with success gates for each stage
  • Operating playbookHow the team runs and supports it day to day
  • Handoff planYour team owns the result after launch
