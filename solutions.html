01 • AI ADOPTION SPRINT

Decide where AI belongs

A focused engagement that replaces scattered ideas with one clear priority, a defensible business case and a practical route into action.

What we do

  • Clarify the business outcome and operating constraint
  • Review candidate workflows, current tools, data and ownership
  • Score opportunities by value, feasibility, risk and return
  • Select the strongest use case and define the next decision
What you receive: priority use case, business case, risk view, success measures and action plan.
02 • ACTIVATE

Turn one priority into working practice

Activate moves a selected use case from intention into a working solution. The workflow, technology, adoption and operating ownership are designed as one system.

What we do

  • Redesign the current workflow around the target outcome
  • Define human review, decision rights and accountable ownership
  • Select or configure the right solution
  • Plan the pilot, rollout, adoption and handoff
What you receive: target workflow, operating model, implementation plan, control points, adoption measures and handoff plan.
03 • ADVANCE

Improve and extend what works

Advance strengthens early AI results and expands them across connected teams, processes and systems without losing ownership, control or measurable value.

What we do

  • Review adoption, quality, exceptions and operating results
  • Resolve workflow, data, integration and ownership gaps
  • Strengthen controls, reporting and review cadence
  • Build the sequence for responsible expansion
What you receive: performance scorecard, improvement backlog, integration priorities and scale plan.
04 • TRANSFORM

Build AI into the operating model

Transform connects strategy, workflows, technology, governance and performance across the organization so AI becomes part of how the business operates.

What we do

  • Shape the enterprise AI portfolio and investment sequence
  • Design the target operating model and decision rights
  • Connect governance, financial oversight and performance measures
  • Establish leadership cadence, adoption plan and ownership transfer
What you receive: transformation roadmap, target operating model, governance structure, investment priorities and executive performance rhythm.
