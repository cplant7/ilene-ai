01 — ASSESS & PRIORITIZE

Know where you are before deciding where to invest.

We create a fact-based view of AI maturity, opportunity and risk so leadership can focus investment where the operating value is strongest.

AI Readiness & Maturity Assessment

Assess how AI actually operates across the business — not simply which tools have been purchased. We look at operational alignment, ownership and accountability, data and systems integration, revenue and efficiency linkage, leadership visibility, and governance and risk controls.

  • Current-state AI use, including unmanaged or shadow AI
  • Workflow, data and integration gaps that block scale
  • Ownership, human accountability and governance maturity
  • Leadership visibility into use, risk and performance
Typical outputs: maturity stage and heat map, priority gaps, risk register, 90-day action plan and leadership briefing.

AI Opportunity & ROI Prioritization

Identify where AI can materially improve a decision, workflow or customer outcome — and where it is unlikely to justify the cost or complexity.

  • Workflow and use-case inventory
  • Value, feasibility and risk scoring
  • Baseline measures for revenue, cost, productivity or customer impact
  • Near-term wins versus longer-term capabilities
Typical outputs: ranked use-case portfolio, preliminary ROI case, implementation sequence and a clear “not now” list.
02 — DESIGN & IMPLEMENT

Redesign the work, not just the tool.

Once the right opportunity is clear, we translate it into a workable operating model: who owns it, how the workflow changes, what technology is required and how the team will use it.

Workflow Redesign & Operating Model

Map the current process, remove unnecessary effort and design the target workflow around the right combination of people, automation and AI.

  • Current-state and target-state workflow design
  • Human-in-the-loop decisions, escalation and exception handling
  • Roles, decision rights and accountable ownership
  • Data flows, system handoffs, approval points and review controls
Typical outputs: target workflow, ownership model, governance approach, control points and KPI definitions.

Vendor Selection & Solution Design

Evaluate solutions against the realities of your environment rather than the strength of a demo. We assess fit across data and security, integration, model transparency, commercial terms, support and internal readiness.

  • Business and technical requirements
  • Data location, privacy, security and model-use questions
  • Integration feasibility and implementation assumptions
  • Pricing, lock-in, exit terms, support model and roadmap
Typical outputs: weighted vendor scorecard, RFP/comparison matrix, recommendation and implementation assumptions.

Implementation, Adoption & Handoff

Turn the design into working practice. We can lead the operating side of implementation directly or work alongside internal teams and selected implementation partners.

  • Pilot and rollout plan with clear success gates
  • Configuration and integration coordination
  • Change, communication and user adoption planning
  • Training, operating documentation and ownership transfer
Typical outputs: implementation roadmap, adoption measures, operating playbook, launch criteria and handoff plan.
03 — MEASURE & GROW

Make the value visible.

AI adoption is not the outcome. We define what success means, track whether the workflow is improving and connect the result back to financial and customer performance.

AI Performance & ROI

Establish the baseline, measures and operating cadence needed to determine whether the investment is creating value.

  • Business case and benefit baseline
  • KPIs across revenue, cost, productivity, margin and customer outcomes
  • Adoption, quality and exception metrics
  • 30/60/90-day performance reviews and optimization backlog
Typical outputs: ROI scorecard, performance dashboard, review cadence and prioritized optimization actions.

Customer & Revenue Performance

Apply AI and operating intelligence across the customer lifecycle to improve service, retention, expansion and delivery performance.

  • Support and service operations
  • Customer success, renewals and account health
  • Professional services, implementation and utilization
  • Revenue operations, forecasting and expansion signals
Typical outputs: lifecycle and signal map, health measures, operating dashboard, retention/expansion actions and performance rhythm.
