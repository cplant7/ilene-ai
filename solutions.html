OUR APPROACH

Adoption starts with the first action

We start with the business outcome, not the tool. Then we rank opportunities by value, effort, data and integration burden, risk and time-to-value so the business can move in the right order.

The objective is not a roadmap that sits on a shelf. It is a working sequence: identify the best three opportunities, action the first, prove the result, then progress to the next level of adoption.

ADOPTION STAGE 01

Activate

Start with the opportunity that can create the strongest measurable return without forcing the business through a major data or systems overhaul first.

AI Adoption Sprint

Build a focused view of the best AI opportunities across the business and determine what should be actioned first.

  • Workflow and friction-point inventory
  • Value, effort, data, risk and time-to-value scoring
  • Top 3 opportunities ranked in the right sequence
  • ROI baseline and decision logic for the first move
Typical outputs: Top 3 AI opportunities, first-move recommendation, preliminary ROI case and a practical action plan.

First Win Implementation

Turn the strongest opportunity into working practice using the systems and data already available wherever possible.

  • Target workflow redesign
  • Solution selection or configuration
  • Ownership, controls and exception handling
  • KPI baseline, launch measures and handoff
Typical outputs: working implementation, operating playbook, KPI baseline, adoption measures and handoff plan.
ADOPTION STAGE 02

Advance

Once the first win is proven, take on the next opportunity where more value depends on stronger integration, better data or a more deliberate operating model.

Workflow, Data & Integration

Remove the constraints that block the next level of adoption without turning the project into a broad transformation program.

  • Current-state and target-state workflow design
  • System handoffs and integration requirements
  • Data quality, access and ownership improvements
  • Human review, approvals, controls and escalation
Typical outputs: target workflow, integration plan, ownership model, data actions, control points and implementation sequence.

Solution Selection & Expanded Implementation

Choose and implement the solution that fits the operating environment, not simply the strongest demo.

  • Business and technical requirements
  • Vendor or platform comparison
  • Commercial, privacy, security and support considerations
  • Implementation, adoption, training and ownership transfer
Typical outputs: weighted solution recommendation, implementation roadmap, adoption plan, success gates and handoff.
ADOPTION STAGE 03

Transform

Use proven adoption to tackle the larger opportunities: cross-functional workflows, structural operating change and higher-value automation that requires more time and coordination.

Operating Model & Workflow Transformation

Redesign how work moves across teams when the opportunity is too large to solve with a single tool or isolated use case.

  • Cross-functional workflow redesign
  • Roles, decision rights and accountable ownership
  • Automation and agentic workflow design
  • Operating cadence, controls and change management
Typical outputs: target operating model, transformed workflow, accountability structure, implementation roadmap and transition plan.

Performance, ROI & Scale

Connect AI adoption to operating and financial performance so leadership can see what is working, what needs adjustment and where to scale next.

  • Revenue, cost, productivity, margin and customer measures
  • Adoption, quality, exception and control metrics
  • Performance dashboards and review cadence
  • Optimization backlog and next-opportunity sequencing
Typical outputs: ROI scorecard, performance dashboard, review cadence, optimization actions and next-stage recommendation.

AI Risk Reduction

Assess risk and recommend practical controls, ownership, escalation paths and monitoring requirements while operational accountability remains with the client.

  • Risk and control review
  • Governance and decision rights
  • Ownership and escalation paths
  • Monitoring and review requirements
Typical outputs: prioritized risk register, recommended controls, governance model, ownership map and monitoring requirements.
