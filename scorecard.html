How the Scorecard works

It rates 6 dimensions separately, then places your organization in one of 4 stages.

6 dimensions

  1. Operational alignmentWhether AI is tied to real workflows and operating priorities, or sits in side projects.
  2. Ownership and accountabilityWhether each AI use has a named owner, and someone answerable when it goes wrong.
  3. Data and systems integrationWhether the data and systems AI depends on are connected, reliable and reachable.
  4. Revenue and efficiency linkageWhether AI moves a measure the business already tracks: revenue, cost, retention or margin.
  5. Leadership visibilityWhether leaders see AI use, risk and results in a way that changes their decisions.
  6. Governance and risk controlsWhether controls, review and escalation cover AI use across the organization.

4 stages

  1. 1
    FragmentedAI shows up in pockets, with no shared standard, owner or link to a business outcome.
  2. 2
    EmergingSome functions have a defined approach, but it has not spread.
  3. 3
    AlignedAI is consistent within functions and tied to a business metric, but not yet across them.
  4. 4
    OptimizedAI is embedded across the organization, with clear ownership, governance and a visible line to performance.
