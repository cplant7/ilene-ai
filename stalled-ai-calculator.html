How the calculation works and why the stall rate is adjustable

The math. Total annual AI spend = active initiatives × average fully loaded annual cost. Spend at risk = total spend × the stall rate assumption. Potentially recoverable value = spend at risk × the recovery assumption. The calculator displays a ±30% directional band because these are planning inputs, not audited forecasts.

Why the default is 50%. There is no single industry wide AI failure rate. Definitions and samples differ. Gartner reported in January 2026 that at least 50% of generative AI projects had been abandoned after proof of concept by the end of 2025. S&P Global Market Intelligence reported organizations scrapping an average of roughly 46% of projects between proof of concept and broad adoption. IDC research reported by CIO in 2026 suggested about half of AI proofs of concept were transitioning to production, a substantial improvement from earlier periods. Treat 50% as a neutral starting point, then replace it with your own portfolio data wherever possible.

On recovery. The recovery percentage is an ilene.ai planning assumption, not a third party statistic. What can actually be recovered depends on use case selection, ownership, data readiness, workflow design, governance, integration and adoption. A structured portfolio review is required to establish a defensible number.
